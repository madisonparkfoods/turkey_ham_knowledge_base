# Planning and Quantities

## Start with the headcount and the oven

Two numbers drive every other decision:

1. **How many people are eating**, split into adults, kids and anyone with dietary needs.
2. **How much oven you have.** A single standard oven fits one large turkey and very little else. That limits how many baked sides you can serve hot.

Write these down first. Then pick the main, then the sides.

## Turkey, ham, or both?

| Situation | Suggestion |
|---|---|
| Up to ~6 people | Turkey breast (bone-in, 5–7 lb) or a small ham. Easier than a whole bird. |
| 8–14 people | One 12–18 lb turkey, or one bone-in ham. |
| 15–20 people | One 18–22 lb turkey, or a 12–14 lb turkey plus a ham. Two smaller birds cook faster and more evenly than one huge one. |
| 20+ people | Two turkeys, or turkey plus ham. Consider borrowing a second oven or roasting one bird a day early and reheating sliced meat in broth. |
| Mixed crowd, some don't eat poultry | Add a spiral ham; it only needs reheating and frees attention. |

**Why not one 25 lb turkey?** The breast overcooks long before the thighs are done, it may not fit your pan or oven, and it is hard to lift safely when hot.

## How many side dishes?

- Plan **one main, one starch, one stuffing/dressing, one or two vegetables, cranberry sauce, gravy, rolls, and dessert.** That is a complete Thanksgiving for most groups.
- Add one side per ~6 extra guests, preferably one a guest brings.
- More dishes does not mean more food eaten; it means smaller portions of each. Use the per-person table in [01-quick-reference.md](01-quick-reference.md) for the totals, and spread them across your dishes.

## Dietary needs

Ask guests a week ahead. Common needs and simple accommodations:

| Need | Easy accommodation |
|---|---|
| Vegetarian | Bake stuffing outside the bird with vegetable stock. Make one hearty vegetable main (stuffed squash, mushroom gallette). Keep a separate gravy (mushroom). |
| Vegan | As above, plus use olive oil instead of butter in at least the potatoes and one vegetable. |
| Gluten-free | Thicken gravy with cornstarch instead of flour; make a GF cornbread stuffing; check broth and spice labels. Madison Park Foods labels its seasonings gluten-free; still read each label. |
| Dairy-free | Olive oil or dairy-free butter in potatoes; check rolls. |
| Nut allergy | Watch pecan pie, green bean toppings, some stuffings; keep serving spoons separate. |
| Low sodium | Note that dry-brined turkey and most hams are salty. Offer plain roasted vegetables and an unsalted rub (Madison Park's Chairman of the Bird rub is sold as salt-free per reseller listings; check the label). |

Label dishes at the table so guests don't have to ask.

## Asking guests to bring things

It is normal and most guests are glad to help. Assign specifics, not "bring something":

- **Good to assign:** a pie, rolls, a salad, wine/drinks, ice, a side that travels well and doesn't need your oven (cold salads, cranberry sauce, slow-cooker dishes).
- **Keep for yourself:** the turkey or ham, gravy, and anything that needs your oven at the last minute.
- Ask guests to bring dishes ready to serve with their own serving utensil.

## Building the shopping list

1. List every recipe.
2. Combine ingredients across recipes (butter, onions, celery, stock add up fast).
3. Check the pantry before buying.
4. Split the list into **shop now** (shelf-stable, frozen turkey, spices) and **shop 1–3 days before** (fresh herbs, produce, dairy, fresh turkey).
5. Common forgotten items: kitchen twine, foil, instant-read thermometer batteries, roasting rack, paper towels, ice, extra butter, chicken/turkey stock, flour or cornstarch for gravy, coffee for after dinner, take-home containers.

## Budgeting time

| When | What |
|---|---|
| 2–3 weeks out | Finalize guest list and menu, order fresh turkey or ham, buy frozen turkey |
| 1 week out | Buy pantry items, confirm guest contributions, check equipment |
| Thaw start date | Move frozen turkey to the fridge (see [04-thawing.md](04-thawing.md)) |
| 1–3 days out | Dry brine, make cranberry sauce, pies, make-ahead gravy, prep vegetables |
| Day before | Assemble casseroles, set the table, chill drinks |
| Day of | Roast, finish sides, gravy, carve |
