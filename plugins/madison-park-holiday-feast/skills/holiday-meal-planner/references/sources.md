## Food safety and cooking times

- USDA FSIS safe minimum internal temperatures (poultry and stuffing 165°F; fresh or cook-before-eating ham 145°F + 3-minute rest; fully cooked ham from USDA-inspected plants reheated to 140°F, others and leftovers to 165°F). Search fsis.usda.gov for "Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart". Also summarized by University of Arkansas Extension: https://www.uaex.uada.edu/life-skills-wellness/extension-homemakers/agent-resources/Keeping%20Safe%20Handout%203.pdf
- USDA FSIS "Let's Talk Turkey" (thawing: refrigerator ~24 h per 4–5 lb, cold water ~30 min per lb; roasting timetable at 325°F; stuffing; frozen turkeys take ~50% longer): https://www.fsis.usda.gov/sites/default/files/media_file/2021-04/Lets_Talk_Turkey.pdf
- USDA FSIS "Ham and Food Safety" (ham types, 325°F cooking/reheating timetable, country ham soaking, storage times; checked 2026-11-01): https://www.fsis.usda.gov/es/node/3302
- USDA FSIS "Leftovers and Food Safety" (2-hour rule, 3–4 day refrigerator storage, reheat to 165°F). Search fsis.usda.gov for the title.

The USDA reorganizes its site periodically; if a link moves, search fsis.usda.gov for the page title. The numbers are long-standing USDA guidance.
