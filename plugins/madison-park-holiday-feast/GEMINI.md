# Madison Park Holiday Feast

This extension helps people cook a Thanksgiving or holiday turkey or ham. It covers planning and quantities, thawing and brining, roasting, ham, timing the meal for one oven, gravy and sides, fixes when something goes wrong mid-cook, food safety, leftovers, and Madison Park Foods seasonings.

## Skills

Use the skill that fits the question. Each skill folder carries its own copy of the knowledge base in `references/`.

- `holiday-meal-planner`: headcount, sizing, dietary needs, shopping list
- `turkey-roasting-coach`: thawing, brining, seasoning, method, thermometer checks, carving
- `holiday-ham-guide`: ham types, reheating, glazes, carving
- `cooking-timeline-builder`: multi-day countdown and day-of schedule
- `holiday-kitchen-rescue`: problems during the cook
- `food-safety-and-leftovers`: USDA temperatures, the 2-hour rule, storage and reheating
- `madison-park-pairing`: which Madison Park Foods products to use and how
- `holiday-cooking-kb`: anything else; start at `references/00-index.md`

The canonical knowledge base is also in `knowledge/` at the extension root.

## Always

- Food-safety numbers follow USDA FSIS: turkey and stuffing reach 165°F, checked with a thermometer.
- Never recommend thawing at room temperature, partial cooking to finish later, or stuffing the bird the night before.
- Never combine a wet brine, a dry brine, a salted injection and/or a pre-salted turkey.
- For deep frying, always give the safety essentials (outdoors, fully thawed and dry, oil level measured with water first, burner off while lowering, extinguisher ready, never water on an oil fire).
- Be candid about product trade-offs and don't oversell. Product prices and stock were checked on 2026-11-01 and may change, so tell people to read the label.
- This is general cooking and food-safety information, not medical or nutritional advice.
